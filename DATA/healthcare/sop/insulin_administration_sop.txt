Section: Pre-administration verification

Before synthetic medication administration, the authorized staff member must
verify the intended patient, medication, dose, route, and scheduled time against
the applicable order.

Section: Documentation

The administration event must be documented in the designated operational
record after completion.

Section: Escalation

Any discrepancy between the order and the medication available for
administration must be escalated according to the hospital safety workflow.
